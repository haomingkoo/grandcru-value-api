    How the score works

    Each offer gets a score out of 100. Higher means a better price for a well-rated wine. Offers we could not match to a Vivino listing are not scored and are listed last.

    PartUp toHow it is counted
    Saving vs Grand Cru30One point for each percent Platinum is cheaper than Grand Cru.
    Saving vs Vivino30One point for each percent Platinum is cheaper than the Vivino market price. This catches bottles that only look cheap because Grand Cru is expensive.
    Vivino rating25The Vivino rating out of 5, scaled to 25.
    Number of ratings10More ratings earn more points, reaching the full 10 at about 1,000 ratings.
    Cheaper than both5Added when Platinum is more than 5% cheaper than both Grand Cru and Vivino.

    Where the prices come from

    Platinum prices and stock come from the Platinum Wine Club shop. Comparison prices come from Grand Cru Wines. Ratings and market prices come from Vivino; for magnums and bundles, the Vivino price is scaled to the same number of bottles.

    We read each wine’s public Vivino page at most once a week, a few pages a day in total, under a name that says who we are, and only where Vivino’s robots.txt allows. We keep the price, rating and number of ratings, show the date each price was checked, and link every Vivino figure back to the wine’s Vivino page. If Vivino stops answering, the page says market prices are paused.

    Prices can change between checks. Confirm the price on the shop’s own page before you buy. This site is independent and is not affiliated with American Express, Platinum Wine Club, Grand Cru Wines or Vivino.
