Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

MinMax Wine is run by Haoming Koo, an individual in Singapore. It is an independent site and is not affiliated with American Express, Platinum Wine Club, Grand Cru Wines or Vivino.

What we collect

Very little. There is no account, no sign-up form and no cookies. The site does not store anything in your browser.

Like most web servers, ours writes a short log line for each request: the time, the page asked for, and your IP address. We use these logs to keep the site running and to block abuse, such as too many requests from one address.

Who sees it

  • Railway hosts the site and keeps the server logs for a limited period.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site. Its Web Analytics counts page views in aggregate, without cookies and without recording what you type.
  • Bottle photos load from the Platinum Wine Club image server, which sees your IP address when your browser fetches them.
  • The origin map loads its background tiles from OpenStreetMap when you scroll to it. OpenStreetMap sees your IP address for those requests; see its privacy policy.

When you follow a link to Platinum Wine Club, Grand Cru Wines, Vivino or Wine-Searcher, their own privacy policies apply.
